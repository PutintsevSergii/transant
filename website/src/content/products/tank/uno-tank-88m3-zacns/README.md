# UNO TANK 88m³ - Zacns

High-capacity tank wagon for efficient and reliable liquid bulk transport.

The UNO TANK 88 m³ Zacns is engineered for the safe and efficient transportation of liquid chemical, petrochemical and petroleum products across European rail networks. With an 88 m³ tank capacity and optimized lightweight design, it combines high carrying capacity with excellent transport efficiency.

The wagon features an L4BH tank code and is engineered in accordance with applicable RID, TSI, GCU, EN and UIC requirements.
