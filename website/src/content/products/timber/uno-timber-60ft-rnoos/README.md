# UNO TIMBER 60ft - Rnoos

High-capacity timber wagon for efficient and secure transport of roundwood and sawn timber.

The UNO TIMBER 60ft Rnoos is designed to maximize efficiency throughout the timber logistics chain. Its lightweight construction combines high payload capacity with optimized loading performance.

The wagon features a robust, optimized stanchion arrangement that ensures reliable cargo retention without additional securing of roundwood. Designed to accommodate 3, 4 and 5 m timber lengths, it provides flexibility for different loading requirements.
