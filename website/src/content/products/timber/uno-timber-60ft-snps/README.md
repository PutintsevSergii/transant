# UNO TIMBER 60ft - Snps

Maximum payload. Optimized timber logistics.

The UNO TIMBER 60ft Snps is engineered to maximize payload efficiency in demanding forestry operations. Its exceptionally low tare weight and high payload capacity enable operators to transport more timber per train.

Designed specifically for roundwood and sawn timber, the wagon combines optimized loading geometry with a robust stanchion system and integrated cargo-securing equipment.
