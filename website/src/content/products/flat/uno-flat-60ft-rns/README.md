# UNO FLAT 60ft - Rns

Heavy-duty capacity. Flexible loading. Reliable transport.

The UNO FLAT 60ft Rns is a specialized flat wagon designed for the safe and efficient transportation of long, heavy and oversized cargo. Its optimized lightweight construction combines high payload capacity with excellent load flexibility.

Equipped with foldable bolsters and stanchions, the wagon can be adapted to different cargo dimensions and loading requirements — supporting steel products, pipes, construction elements and long industrial components.
