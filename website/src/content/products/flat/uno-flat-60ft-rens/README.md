# UNO FLAT 60ft - Rens

One wagon. Multiple cargo solutions.

The UNO FLAT 60ft Rens is a versatile flat wagon designed for the efficient transportation of steel products, timber, construction materials and general cargo. Its optimized lightweight design combines high payload capacity with excellent load flexibility.

The wagon features a spacious 60ft loading platform with foldable side walls and swivel stanchions, enabling secure and efficient transport of a wide range of long, bulky and oversized loads.
