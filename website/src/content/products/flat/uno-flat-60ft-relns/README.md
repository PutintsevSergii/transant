# UNO FLAT 60ft - Relns

More capacity. Greater flexibility. Efficient freight transport.

The UNO FLAT 60ft Relns is a high-capacity flat wagon designed for the efficient transportation of general and industrial cargo. Its lightweight construction and spacious loading area combine high payload capacity with excellent operational flexibility.

Equipped with foldable side walls and a robust wooden floor, the wagon provides flexible cargo handling and reliable support for a wide range of freight.
